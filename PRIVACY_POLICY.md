# Privacy Policy for WikiChanges

**Last Updated: August 4, 2026**

WikiChanges ("the App") is an Android application designed to help users review and patrol recent changes on Wikipedia projects. This policy explains how we handle your information.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

### Authentication (OAuth)
To perform actions on Wikipedia (such as patrolling or rolling back edits), the App uses the official Wikimedia OAuth2 system. 
- **What we store**: The App stores your access and refresh tokens locally on your device.
- **Why**: These tokens are used to identify you to the Wikimedia API so you can perform edits under your own account.
- **Email**: While the App may fetch your account details (like username and groups) to display your profile in the Settings menu, **we do not store your email address** in any persistent way, nor do we transmit it to any servers other than the official Wikimedia API.

### Local Storage
All app-specific data, including your selected wikis, custom filters, and login tokens, is stored **locally on your device** using Android's secure DataStore mechanism. We do not have any central servers, and we cannot access your local data.

## 2. Third-Party Services

### Wikimedia Foundation
The App communicates directly with the official Wikimedia API (e.g., `wikipedia.org`). When you use the App, your IP address and standard device information (such as your User-Agent) are transmitted to Wikimedia's servers as part of the standard HTTP request process. Your use of these services is governed by the [Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not share, sell, or trade your personal data with any third parties. There is no background tracking, analytics, or advertising included in the App.

## 4. Data Retention
Because all data is stored locally on your device, it remains there as long as the App is installed. You can delete all data at any time by:
1. Using the "Log out" button in the App's settings.
2. Clearing the App's storage/cache in your Android system settings.
3. Uninstalling the App.

## 5. Security
We use industry-standard security practices (such as HTTPS and PKCE for OAuth) to ensure that your communication with Wikipedia is secure and your tokens are handled safely.
